# Analysis of these images

- the images are 180x240
- the distance between the camera and light pole increases as the images
  progress from 1-6. In image 1, the light pole nearly fills the frame
  vertically. in image 6, its height is roughly 1/8 of the frame.
- there are dark gaps between each LED on the light pole. These are easily
  visible at all distances.
- there is light from the light pole reflecting from the table in images 3-6.
  When put through hue/brightness gates, the reflection appears as a big blob.
- behind the light pole, there is a window with a blind covering it. There is
  light coming around the sides of the blind, causing bright, solid horizontal
  and vertical lines.
